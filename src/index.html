What I do

Things I Work On

Most of my experience is in machine learning, but I also build web apps and have worked on embedded systems. Working in small teams has meant doing a fair bit outside my job title.

  • I’ve built a subtitling platform using Claude, Gemini, GPT, and Deepgram, with people reviewing the output. I also built an LLM assistant used by 200 students daily.

  • Mostly Django: automating manual work, integrating payments, and building internal tools. I also handle deployment and cloud infrastructure.

  • Computer vision, time series, and physics-informed neural networks. I’ve worked on battery life prediction, wildfire detection, and modelling airborne microplastics. Mostly in PyTorch.

  • I’ve built pipelines for satellite imagery and models for emissions and land classification, using tools like Rasterio, GeoPandas, Spark, and AWS.

  • At Lux Aerobot, I built the balloon gondola hardware and firmware, and got computer vision models running on NVIDIA Jetson. There was a fair bit of fieldwork too.
